
# 📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard

A **Power BI Sales Analytics Dashboard** built to analyze customer behavior, order trends, product performance, and city-wise sales. This dashboard enables businesses to uncover **time-based insights**, optimize sales strategies, and improve decision-making.

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard focuses on analyzing **Customers, Orders, and Products** to provide:

* Real-time **sales performance tracking**
* Customer and product-level insights
* City-wise and category-wise analysis
* Time-based trends for forecasting and growth

---

## 📌 Key Metrics (KPIs)

* 📦 **Total Quantity:** 56K
* 👥 **Total Customers:** 500
* 🧾 **Total Orders:** 10K
* 💰 **Total Sales:** 6.72 Million
* 📊 **Average Order Value:** 662.33

## 📈 Features & Analysis

### 🕒 Time-Based Analysis

* Tracks sales trends across **Year, Quarter, Month, Day**
* Identifies **peak demand periods**
* Helps in **forecasting future sales**

### 🛍️ Product Performance

* Sales distribution across product categories:

  * Electronics
  * Fashion
  * Sports
  * Footwear
  * Health

### 🌍 City-Wise Sales

* Highlights top-performing cities like:

  * Delhi
  * Mumbai
  * Chennai
* Helps identify **regional demand patterns**

### 👥 Customer Insights

* Tracks customer purchase behavior
* Identifies repeat buying patterns

---

## 💡 Key Insights

### 📍 Timing Drives Growth

* ⏱️ **Peak Demand Periods:** Identify high-sales time windows
* 🔄 **Customer Behavior Cycles:** Detect recurring buying patterns
* 📊 **Forecasting Accuracy:** Predict demand using historical data
* 🎯 **Personalized Offers:** Target customers based on order timing

---

## ⚠️ Key Observations

* Sales fluctuate significantly across years
* Certain categories dominate revenue contribution
* City-wise performance shows uneven distribution
* Order timing plays a critical role in sales growth

---

## 🎯 Strategic Recommendations

* ✅ Optimize marketing during **peak demand periods**
* ✅ Use time-based data for **better forecasting**
* ✅ Personalize offers based on customer behavior
* ✅ Focus on high-performing cities for expansion
* ✅ Improve low-performing categories

## 🧠 Business Takeaway

> Treat **Order Date** not just as a record, but as a **predictive signal**.
> Businesses leveraging time-based analytics can significantly improve performance and customer retention.
